The internet in one word: **brainrot**.

Eight letters, one beat. It is what the whole internet does to a
brain left alone with it for an afternoon — it rots it, lovingly,
in public, with friends watching. Funny on its own, true of the
internet as a whole, and not the first obvious word anyone reaches
for.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. The readable
source is `line_src.py`; `main.py` is the `makefun compress` pack
of that source.
